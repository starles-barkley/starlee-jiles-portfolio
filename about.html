About

I didn't come into technology with only a technical education.

I brought years of experience working directly with people — paperwork, deadlines, administrative systems, customers, and organizations.

During my roughly four collective years as a legal assistant, I learned a law firm's day-to-day work closely enough to see where technology could remove repetitive effort. After completing my full-stack software development education, I built custom document-generation software for Coffman & Seidenberger, PLLC. I continue to maintain and improve the firm's applications as a contract software engineer.

I've since moved into a full-time technology career in healthcare. Along with my experience in legal services, healthcare, retail, delivery work, church leadership and service, and software, this has shown me that every organization operates a little differently.

I want to build solutions that make sense to the people who will actually use them.
