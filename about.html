About

Technology is only part of what I bring.

I brought years of experience working directly with people, paperwork, deadlines, administrative systems, customers, and organizations.

Across four years of legal administrative work, I learned how closely technology and day-to-day operations affect one another. While working in the legal field and completing my full-stack software-development education, I began building custom applications to address processes I understood firsthand. That included document-generation software for Coffman & Seidenberger, PLLC, whose applications I continue to maintain and improve as a contract software engineer.

I have since moved into a full-time technology career in healthcare. Along with my experience in legal services, healthcare, retail, delivery work, church leadership and service, and software, this has taught me that every organization operates differently.

I build solutions that make sense to the people who will actually use them.
